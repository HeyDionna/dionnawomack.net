Talk to Elara

Open the full demo

I designed Elara as a sidekick for Dungeon Masters: a persona with her own voice, art and moods, intent matching for the questions DMs actually ask, three reply variants per intent, and fallback lines that point you somewhere useful instead of a dead end.

She knows my work too. Ask her about PixelPlay, Tenor or how she was made. The fallback line gets read more than any other line, so it got the most care.

Well met. I'm Elara. Dionna built me for D&D, but I know her work too. What do you want to know?

PixelPlay

Play the prototype

Girls with strong visual skills get told tech means coding. PixelPlay turns the dress-up games I grew up on into real design lessons: every colour choice, layout and component placement teaches a UX principle.

Three age tiers, 44px touch targets, no time limits, no dark patterns, COPPA-ready. The goal: prove that making things pretty is strategic, empathetic work.

Vie, then the Zine Room

Enter the Zine Room

Vie started as a concept: a listening party for Doja Cat's album where each love language gets its own room, with RFID wristbands, listening pods, a projection stage and a companion app.

Then I built it. The Zine Room is a WebVR piece in A-Frame: pick your love language, walk its room, and leave with a photo from the booth.

CyFire.ai

Visit CyFire.ai

I consult on the UX, UI and AI parts of CyFire's crisis-readiness platform. I run QA, file bugs with screenshots, and write up suggestions: an accessibility report, a performance report, a page restructure.

One of my notes: the AI coach kept giving the same unhelpful answer. I suggested scripted replies or a token budget for more varied responses, the same problem I solved for Elara.

Teaching AI what good looks like

At gTech I co-write the rubrics and guidelines used to judge AI-generated shopping and text ads, published three ways: a deck, a guidance doc and a decision tree. I run training, consensus and feedback sessions for more than 80 raters across vendor teams and help settle the edge cases.

I also build the tools around the work: a searchable task library with version control, Apps Script automations that cut manual coordination by 30%, a card-based web app that replaced a spreadsheet 50 people were editing at once, and training videos made in Google Vids. The details are confidential; the method isn't.
